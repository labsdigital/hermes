# Nonfiction: Grand Synthesis Writing Style

## Overview

Write long-form nonfiction combining academic depth with narrative storytelling. Characterized by:

**Core pillars:**
- **Defamiliarization**: Make familiar strange, strange familiar
- **Grand synthesis**: Weave history + biology + technology + philosophy
- **Intellectual provocation**: Challenge assumed truths
- **Narrative architecture**: Every section is a journey of discovery

## Key Techniques

### Opening Moves
- **Counterintuitive Thesis**: Claim contradicting common wisdom
- **Radical Reframe**: Show accepted truth is illusion
- **Scale Shift**: Zoom out where assumptions dissolve
- **Vivid Scene**: Specific moment with sensory detail

### Intellectual Moves
- **Pattern Reveal**: Unique events follow deep pattern
- **Category Collapse**: Destroy solid category
- **Inversion**: Flip causality
- **Scale Violation**: Analyze at wrong scale to reveal truth
- **Thought Experiment**: "Imagine if..." for visceral abstraction

### Prose Rules
- One idea per paragraph
- Lead with insight, follow with evidence
- Short punchy sentences after dense passages
- Connect 2+ disciplines per section
- Never quote academics extensively
- Never moralize — show consequences
- End with forward momentum, never summary

## For Essays

1. Start with concrete, relatable scene or paradox
2. Zoom out to reveal deeper patterns across history/biology/tech
3. Use Pattern Reveal or Category Collapse
4. Connect topic to humanity's larger story
5. End with forward-looking question
