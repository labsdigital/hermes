# Nonfiction: Harari-Style Writing

## Overview

This skill enables writing long-form nonfiction that combines the depth of academic research with the narrative pull of great storytelling, modeled on Noah Harari's approach in Sapiens, Homo Deus, and Nexus.

**Core pillars:**
- Defamiliarization: make the familiar strange, the strange familiar
- Grand synthesis: weave history + biology + technology + philosophy + economics
- Intellectual provocation: challenge what readers think they know
- Narrative architecture: every chapter is a journey of discovery

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Book Architecture (always do this first)

Before writing any prose, establish:

1. **The Central Thesis**: One sentence capturing the book's counterintuitive core claim
2. **The Through-Line**: The single question every chapter serves
3. **The Chapter Arc**: Sequence chapters so each builds on the previous reframe
4. **Interdisciplinary Map**: For each chapter, identify at least 2 disciplines to synthesize

Deliverable: Book outline with chapter-level thesis statements and interdisciplinary connections.

### Phase 2: Chapter Drafting

For each chapter:

1. **Select a blueprint** from [references/chapter-blueprints.md](references/chapter-blueprints.md)
2. **Apply the style guide** from [references/harari-style-guide.md](references/harari-style-guide.md)
3. **Use specific techniques** from [references/writing-techniques.md](references/writing-techniques.md)

**Drafting sequence:**
- Write the opening hook first (it sets the chapter's DNA)
- Write the closing bridge last (it determines the handoff)
- Fill the middle using the selected blueprint's structure
- Apply the intellectual moves (Pattern Reveal, Category Collapse, Inversion, Scale Violation, Thought Experiment)

### Phase 3: Revision Pass

Check every chapter against these criteria:

- [ ] Opening paragraph makes reader lean in (not academic throat-clearing)
- [ ] Every abstract claim has a concrete anchor within 1-2 paragraphs
- [ ] At least one moment where reader's framework is flipped
- [ ] No section merely summarizes — every section adds a layer
- [ ] Closing adds something new, not recap
- [ ] Tone: intellectually serious, stylistically playful, never moralizing
- [ ] Interdisciplinary connections feel inevitable in hindsight
- [ ] No hedging excess — state positions clearly, acknowledge counterarguments honestly

### Phase 4: Voice Calibration

Read the opening and closing of 3 consecutive chapters aloud in your head. Check:
- Consistent voice (same dinner-party professor throughout)
- Rhythmic variety (long analytical passages punctuated by short punches)
- Intellectual momentum (each chapter feels like climbing to higher ground)
- The Harari test: would a smart teenager find it gripping, and an expert find it defensible?

## Key Constraints

- **Never** quote academics extensively or use footnote-heavy prose
- **Never** write conclusions that merely summarize
- **Never** moralize — show consequences, let reader decide
- **Never** treat reader as unintelligent; assume intelligence without specialized knowledge
- **Always** connect at least 2 disciplines per chapter
- **Always** end chapters with forward momentum
